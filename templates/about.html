About FindMe

Cavendish University Uganda's AI-Powered Lost and Found Platform

Our Mission

FindMe helps the Cavendish University community report, search, match, verify and recover lost property efficiently and securely.

Security & Privacy

Sensitive information is protected until an administrator approves a match. Contact details are never exposed publicly without verification.

Technology

Built with Python, Flask, MySQL, and an AI-assisted matching engine designed for academic demonstration.

How It Works

From report to recovery in four simple steps

1

Report

Report lost or found items with details and images.

2

Match

AI analyzes reports and identifies potential matches.

3

Verify

Administrators review and approve matches.

4

Recover

Owners receive recovery information to collect items.
